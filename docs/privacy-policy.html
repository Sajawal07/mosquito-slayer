100% Offline Game • Zero Data Collection

Mosquito Slayer does not collect, record, track, transmit, or share any personal information, location, device ID, or user telemetry. All game data stays strictly on your local device.

1 Overview & Game Description

Mosquito Slayer is an offline casual action arcade game developed by Muhammad Sajawal. In this game, players hunt down buzzing mosquitoes using various unlockable weapons (Hand, Slipper, Electric Mosquito Racket).

This privacy document explains our transparent commitment to your privacy and outlines the terms governing the use of the game on mobile phones, tablets, and compatible Android devices.

2 Zero Personal Data Collection

We believe that gaming should be private and hassle-free. Mosquito Slayer operates with a strict No-Data-Collection policy:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or age.
  • No Location Tracking: We never access GPS or network-based geographical location.
  • No Device Fingerprinting: We do not read IMEI, MAC addresses, SIM information, or phone numbers.
  • No Analytics or Crash Trackers: No third-party analytics (like Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, or Facebook SDK) are embedded in this game.

3 Local Data Storage (Offline Only)

All your in-game progression—including your highest score, accumulated coins, unlocked levels, selected weapon, and audio preferences—is saved purely inside your device's private local storage (HTML5 LocalStorage / Android WebView cache).

This data is never uploaded to any remote server or cloud database. If you uninstall the app or clear its cache, your local game data will be reset.

4 Children & Family Privacy Compliance

Mosquito Slayer is a family-friendly game designed for players of all ages. Because the game collects NO personal data whatsoever, it is fully compliant with:

  • The Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).
  • The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).
  • Google Play's Families Policy and Designed for Families guidelines.

5 Advertisements & Third-Party SDKs

The current release of Mosquito Slayer is completely ad-free and contains no external advertising SDKs or third-party behavioral trackers.
